# Institution Privacy Responsibilities v1

**This is a concise, customer-facing summary, not legal advice.** It is
designed to support an APP-aligned (Australian Privacy Principles)
privacy approach. Institutions must confirm the privacy, education,
records-management, and other legal requirements that apply to them —
those requirements vary by jurisdiction, sector, and institution type,
and this document does not determine them. See
[`docs/privacy-and-evidence-retention-v1.md`](privacy-and-evidence-retention-v1.md)
for the full internal governance package this summarises.

## What your institution decides

- **Which integrity features are appropriate** for a given exam —
  camera monitoring, AI camera integrity checks, camera/screen evidence
  stills, screen-share requirements, and answer-development provenance
  are all off by default and must be deliberately enabled per exam.
- **Your lawful authority/basis** for using the features you enable, and
  for collecting the personal information they involve.
- **Student notification requirements** beyond Tether's own built-in
  student-facing notice — your institution may need to provide
  additional notice under your own policy or applicable law.
- **Whether optional camera/screen evidence capture should be enabled**
  for a given exam, weighed against the additional sensitivity of image
  evidence.
- **Academic-integrity review and appeals** — Tether never makes a
  misconduct determination; your institution's review and appeal
  process is the decision-maker.
- **Who is an authorised reviewer** for integrity evidence — access is
  scoped to the exam's owning lecturer and your institution's platform
  administrators; your institution controls who holds those roles.
- **Your retention period** for integrity evidence and assessment
  records — see the retention classes in
  `docs/privacy-and-evidence-retention-v1.md`, Section 18.
- **Legal/records-management requirements** that apply to your
  institution's use of student assessment data.
- **Accommodation and accessibility processes** for students who cannot
  or should not be subject to a particular monitoring feature.
- **Student questions and complaints** about how their exam data was
  used.
- **Legal holds** — identifying when a record must be preserved beyond
  the ordinary retention period, and notifying Tether's support channel
  when a hold affects data Tether holds on your behalf.
- **Incident escalation contacts** on your institution's side, for any
  incident that involves your students' data.

## What Tether is responsible for

- Operating the product controls you configure exactly as documented —
  no optional monitoring feature is ever silently enabled.
- Tenant/institution isolation — your data is never visible to another
  institution.
- Protecting held service data using the safeguards actually
  implemented (see the data register for specifics — this document does
  not claim protections beyond what is verifiably built).
- Presenting an accurate, current student-facing notice at
  `/privacy/student-exam-notice`.
- Preserving the human-review boundary — Tether does not and will not
  make automatic misconduct determinations.
- Supporting agreed operational deletion/offboarding processes for your
  institution's data, once those processes are formally agreed.
- Incident escalation, once the separate Incident/NDB Runbook exists —
  see `docs/privacy-and-evidence-retention-v1.md`, Section 23.

## What this document does not do

It does not assign "controller"/"processor" (or equivalent) roles to
either party — that depends on contract terms not yet settled. It does
not claim a specific legal basis applies to your use of Tether. It does
not claim any specific overseas-disclosure position — see the
subprocessor/cross-border register in
`docs/privacy-and-evidence-retention-v1.md`, Section 24, which remains a
pre-pilot gate until populated with verified facts.
